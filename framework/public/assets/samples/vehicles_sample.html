Field Descriptions:

  • Make Name: Vehicle manufacturer (BMW, Audi, Mercedes-Benz, etc.)
  • Model Name: Vehicle model (X5, A4, C-Class, etc.)
  • Year: Manufacturing year (2022, 2023, etc.)
  • Engine Type: Fuel type (Petrol, Diesel, Electric, Hybrid)
  • Color Name: Vehicle color (Black, White, Silver, etc.)
  • VIN: Vehicle Identification Number (17 characters)
  • License Plate: Registration number
  • Horse Power: Engine power in HP
  • Initial Mileage: Starting mileage in miles
  • Vehicle Type ID: 1=Convertible, 2=Coupe, 3=Estate, 4=Hatchback, 5=Minibus, 6=MPV, 7=Pickup, 8=Saloon, 9=SUV
  • Group ID: Vehicle group (usually 1 for default)
  • Vehicle Price: Rental price per period (with insurance included)
  • Insurance Discount: Discount amount when driver chooses without insurance (e.g., if price is £400/week and insurance discount is £50, driver pays £350/week without insurance)
  • Initial Cost: Purchase cost of the vehicle
  • Price Period: monthly or weekly
  • Vehicle Scheme: Rental, Rent To Buy, or Other
  • Vehicle Status: Available, Rented, Workshop, or Disabled
  • Telematics Link: GPS tracking URL
  • Fuel Efficiency: MPG for petrol/diesel, mi/kWh for electric
  • MOT Expiry Day/Month/Year: MOT test expiration date
  • Height/Length/Breadth: Vehicle dimensions in mm
  • Weight: Vehicle weight in kg
